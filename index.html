The nine nights

How to play and win
  • Drag a doll up from the box onto its step, or tap the doll and then tap the step.
  • The order climbs from simple life to the divine: plants → small creatures → animals → birds → people → saints → avatars → gods.
  • You win a night when every doll is on the right step before the guests arrive.
  • ★★★ no mistakes and no hints · ★★ up to 3 mistakes · ★ any finish. Win a night to unlock the next.
  • Points: 100 a doll × combo (up to ×8), +150 a lit lamp, +200 for a trivia answer, catching the mouse or shooing the cat, +250 a star, plus a speed bonus.
  • Chaos: Ganesha's mouse runs past, the neighbour's cat knocks dolls off, and the power goes out. Move your finger to use the torch.
