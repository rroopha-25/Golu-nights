## What is it?

During Navaratri, families set up a **golu**: dolls arranged on steps, climbing from plants and tiny creatures at the bottom all the way up to the gods at the top.

In **Golu Nights**, you drag each doll onto the right step. Sounds easy, until the neighbour's cat walks in, the power goes out, and the guests are ringing the doorbell. 😅

## Why it's fun

### 👵 Meet Pinky Aunty
Every society has one. She inspects your golu and has an opinion on everything.

- Put a doll on the right step: *"Sharmaji's son could never."*
- Put it on the wrong one: *"Hai Ram! Snail up there? Promotion is not that easy."*

### 🌙 Nine nights, nine levels
Each night is its own level, in that night's Navaratri colour. It starts with 3 easy steps and grows to the full 9-step golu. Win a night to unlock the next, and finish Night 9 for a **Dussehra fireworks show**. 🎆

### 🧠 Aunty's quiz
Every time you fill a step, its diya lights up and Aunty asks a quick question about Navaratri, Dussehra, Garba, Durga Puja and more. Get it right for bonus points.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Made for kids *and* grown-ups
- **Kids:** extra time, no points lost for mistakes, friendly hints and easy questions.
- **Grown-ups:** mistakes and hints cost points, step names can be hidden, and the questions get tough.

### 🎨 Make it your own golu
Choose the number of steps, dolls per step, time limit, which chaos events happen, the colour of the step cloth, and which dolls go in the box.

You can even **make your own doll**, like *Nani's tiger from the 1998 Dussehra mela*, and it joins every game.

## 🏆 How to win

**Put every doll on its correct step before the guests arrive.**

The order always climbs from simple life to the divine:

> 🌼 Plants → 🐌 Tiny creatures → 🐢 Crawlers → 🐘 Animals → 🦚 Birds → 💃 People → 📿 Saints → 🏹 Avatars → 🪔 Gods

| Stars | What you need |
|:---:|---|
| ⭐⭐⭐ | No mistakes and no hints |
| ⭐⭐ | Up to 3 mistakes |
| ⭐ | Finish the golu |

Win a night to unlock the next one. Collect all **27 stars** to become a Golu Legend.

### Points

| Do this | Get |
|---|---|
| Place a doll on the right step | **100 × your combo** (combo goes up to ×8) |
| Fill a step and light its diya | **+150** |
| Answer Aunty's question | **+200** |
| Catch the mouse or shoo the cat | **+200** |
| Each star you earn | **+250** |
| Finish fast | **Speed bonus** |

Your points add up across games and move you up the ranks, from *Golu newbie* to *Golu legend*. There are **16 trophies** to unlock, like *Ekdum perfect*, *Shoo billi* and *Light aa gayi*.

## 🎮 How to play

1. **Drag** a doll up from the box onto its step, or tap the doll, then tap the step.
2. Get it right and Aunty cheers. Get it wrong and she lets you know.
3. Fill every step before the guests arrive.
